01	Affidavit	Format	Explained
Affidavit	in	Reply	—	Bombay	High	Court,	writ	matter,	short	reply
Dated:	05-09-2026
An	
Affidavit	in	Reply
	is	a	sworn	statement	filed	by	a	respondent	to	answer	and	oppose	a	petition.	It	denies	the	petitioner's	case,	states	the	respondent's
version,	and	asks	the	Court	to	dismiss	the	petition.	It	follows	a	fixed	skeleton,	and	almost	every	sentence	uses	a	set	phrase.
1.	The	ten	parts
All	ten	appear,	in	this	order.	None	are	optional.

2.	Deponent	rule	(Part	6)
Respondent	is	a	person
	→	that	person	deposes:	“the	Respondent	No.2	above	named”.
Respondent	is	a	company	or	authority
	→	an	officer	deposes	for	it:	“the	[designation]	of	the	Respondent	No.2	above	named”.	Never	write	“I	am	the
Respondent	No.2”	for	an	organisation.
Verb	agreement:	
solemnly	affirm
	→	
Solemnly	affirmed
;	
swear	and	affirm

Sworn

Para
Move
Opening	words

Blanket	denial
“At	the	outset,	I	deny	each	and	every	allegation,	contention	and	submission	made	in	the	Writ	Petition,	save	and

Closing

Two	rules	that	must	never	break
1.	Verification	verb	in	Part	6	matches	the	jurat	verb	in	Part	9.
2.	Paragraph	range	in	Part	10	matches	the	actual	number	of	body	paragraphs.	If	the	body	has	6	paragraphs	it	reads	“paragraphs	1	to	6”.	Copying	“1	to	5”
from	a	sample	is	an	error.
